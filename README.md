## 📖 Project Overview

The **DMART Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using **Power BI** to analyze retail sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, marketing effectiveness, and operational efficiency.

The dashboard transforms raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights through KPI-driven analytics, interactive visualizations, and dynamic filtering capabilities. It enables stakeholders to monitor sales trends, evaluate marketing performance, understand customer demographics, and optimize business operations through data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and revenue performance.
- Track order volume and average order value.
- Evaluate marketing and advertisement effectiveness.
- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
- Measure product category contribution to total revenue.
- Assess discount impact on sales performance.
- Track order delivery, returns, and cancellations.
- Visualize geographic sales distribution across regions.
